Replace the per-line obligation extraction's output with claims: one per sentence or line as the parser already splits them, verbatim, with an id and a line anchor. No model call constructs them. One model call decides, per parser unit, whether the unit feeds claim construction (anything that is not background, motivation, history or references); an uncertain unit feeds it.

Review-state gets a claim record and a reference type that lets an obligation or an expectation cite a list of claims or obligations. The obligation record gets a `claims` field, empty until synthesis (issue 2 in the sequence) fills it. This is the schema later issues depend on, which is why it goes first.
