## What two parked experiments want from M8.4, and what they give it

Both are in `docs/experiments/`, both reached a result, and both stop at the same
wall: a small set of pairs where a static signal and the pair judge disagree,
which nothing static can adjudicate. Injection is the adjudicator.

**They give M8.4 its test selection.** `coverage-prefilter/FINDINGS.md` argues
the coverage map is the right way to choose which tests to run under injection —
a test that never executes a line cannot fail on a mutation of it — at roughly 20
CPU-minutes and zero tokens, against $6.02 of static judging per run. Its 9-of-48
judge-everything fallbacks (documentation defects, absence defects) stay on the
fallback.

**They give it a ready-made first case list.** 43 pairs where coverage and the
judge disagree, plus 3 more from `prefilter-committee/`. Adjudicating those
measures pair-verdict accuracy against ground truth **for the first time** —
#315's human-reviewed labels are the only current proxy — and settles both
experiments at once.

Neither experiment runs from a clean checkout without external inputs; each names
what it needs and stops with a sentence when it is missing. See
`prefilter-committee/paths.py`.

## What PR #328 left unmeasured, and why it matters here

**Whether the pair stage now caches anything.** Every prompt in the
pair-response-shape pilot is under the 1,024-token floor, so nothing there could
show it. It needs one real `acceptance check --mode record`, about $7 at #316's
size. The human is holding on that spend. #324's second acceptance item stays
open until it runs, so **check #324 is not sitting closed with that item unmet.**

**The shape of the rectangle at a fixed judgement budget.** `_batches` now groups
up to `DEFAULT_TESTS_PER_BATCH = 4` tests per call while `DEFAULT_PAIR_BATCH_SIZE`
still caps judgements at 40, so more tests means fewer defects offered with each.
The pilot measured the direction — several tests beat one, 0.9688 against 0.8785
over nine draws each — but never varied the trade at a fixed budget, and its
calls carried at most 15 judgements where production carries 40. **4 is the
smallest step that gets the measured effect, not a tuned value.**
