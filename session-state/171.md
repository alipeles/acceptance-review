## What the record decides

Eight decisions. The two that change other people's work:

- **The defect enumeration stage does not change.** #171 was filed as a decision
  that had to land before M8 starts, because the answer might have forced the
  enumeration stage's output shape to change and orphaned its recordings. It does
  not. `defects/enumeration.py` and its response schema are untouched.
- **`PairVerdict` gains a tier field**, so injection overwrites individual static
  verdicts rather than running as a parallel stage with its own record type. That
  keeps one rating implementation: `defects/support.py::derive_support` counts
  defects with at least one killing verdict and is unchanged by the decision.

The rest: the descriptor is one contiguous span replacement produced at M8.4;
validity is four mechanical checks with no confirming model call; a defect no
candidate test executes is decided at the coverage tier without injecting
anything; one instrumented baseline run at head serves three purposes; every
attempt is recorded with a typed outcome.

## Two things worth not re-deriving

**The switch-arm question was asked and answered.** Where a defect can only occur
on one path no test drives, we do not force execution and do not substitute a
double. The record gives three reasons. A recorded limit: line coverage is too
coarse for a defect on an unexercised branch of a line that does execute, so #44
(M8.3, the coverage-confirmed tier) should record whether branch coverage is on.

**Injection does not fully replace the static pair judgement, and the remainder
is measured.** Three classes stay static permanently on any repository:
defects with no usable code region (9 of 48 at #316's Gate 2), tests that fail
through file reads rather than execution, and absence defects. Separate from
repositories where execution is unavailable, which is §8.3's case.

## Numbers worth not re-deriving

- **#316's Gate 2:** $6.87, 1,012 calls, 992 of them pair judgements, 30
  criteria, 48 defects, 496 candidate tests, 23,808 pairs, 268 kills — a 1.1%
  kill rate. Head `3e1d3a9`. DR-171 rests on this run throughout.
- **Coverage over that run:** the median filtered defect is reachable by 137 of
  the 496 tests; the suite runs at ~0.2s per test; one instrumented pass took
  5m15s. So 48 injections is on the order of 20 CPU-minutes and zero tokens,
  against $6.02 of static pair judging.
- **#314's Gate 2:** $4.25, 375 calls, 37 criteria, 75 defects, 166 candidate
  tests, 12,450 pairs, 127 kills.
- Pricing: prompt $0.75/M, output $4.50/M.
- **Nothing caches under about 1,024 prompt tokens.** Of 5,115 recorded calls,
  all 1,246 under that size cached nothing.
- **Defect-id aliasing does not work.** Per-batch short ids cut output 25% and
  lost a third of the labelled kills. Do not retry.

## One thing PR #328 shipped that is not tuned

`DEFAULT_TESTS_PER_BATCH = 4` in `defects/pair_mapping.py` is the smallest step
that gets a measured effect, not a tuned value. `DEFAULT_PAIR_BATCH_SIZE` still
caps judgements per response at 40, so more tests per call means fewer defects
offered with each — and that trade was never varied.
