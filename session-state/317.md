## Decided while building, not covered by the Gate 1 plan

1. **`requirement_disposition` is singular.** The mandate says a step "returns
   exactly one account", and a list cannot say that. This also retires #217's
   repeated-disposition abort, which is the crash in #298/#313.
2. **An uncovered span that yields nothing raises.** It was reached only because
   the summary step argued with a counterexample that the property is not
   required, so a step that then declines it has contradicted the one that sent
   it. An open question counts as yielding.
3. **`scan` checks `requirement_id` only, not `source_quote`.** Otherwise one bad
   quotation files two unusable-answer records for the same event.
4. **`_in_registry_order` runs on every run and drops nothing.** The summary's
   obligations are derived last and the summary sits first in the registry.
5. **The summary's span-by-span account is on its disposition whatever the
   disposition is**, not only when it yielded nothing. The old path recorded a
   covered property as a merge; deriving no duplicate is the improvement, and
   losing the record of why would be a regression hidden inside it.
