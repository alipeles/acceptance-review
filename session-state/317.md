## The defect — READ `docs/experiments/317-over-answering/findings.md` FIRST

**The paragraph that stood here was wrong, and the correction matters enough that
the old text is quoted rather than deleted.** It said:

> the model split one requirement across twelve dispositions instead of nesting
> twelve obligations inside one disposition's `more_obligations`

That reads the failure as a *shape* problem about one requirement. It is not.
`findings.md` §1, measured over the 1,748 recorded transcripts, shows entries
3–13 quote `constraint-01` … `constraint-12` verbatim, one per constraint, in
registry order. **The model answered for the whole Constraints section**; the
`requirement_id` enum, being the only constrained field, collapsed eleven
distinct intended labels onto the one value it was allowed to write.

What follows from that, and none of it followed from the old reading:

- **The disposition count is a symptom, not the defect.** Six of the eight
  over-answering calls in the corpus returned the *right* number of dispositions
  and carried the foreign obligations inside `more_obligations` instead. Those do
  not crash; they misattribute in silence, which is worse.
- **The merge direction #317's body proposes repairs the wrong thing.** Merging
  entries 2–13 gives `task-01` sixteen obligations, eleven of which
  `_resolve_attributions` re-files onto constraints another batch already derived
  — manufacturing eleven twins to avoid an abort.
- **The trigger is the `# Task` paragraph.** 8 of 35 batches containing a `task-*`
  requirement over-answer, against 0 of 68 without one (Fisher one-sided
  p = 0.0001). The Constraints and Completion sections *are* its elaboration, so
  "what obligations does this paragraph impose" has no bounded answer.
- **A prompt change costs exactly what a schema change costs**, because
  `request_key` hashes the whole request, messages included. There is no cheap
  prompt fix; only post-response fixes are free.

`findings.md` §5 proposes the free one: an obligation whose quotation resolves
inside a requirement this call was not asked to answer for is not this call's
work, so it is dropped and recorded. Applied to the failing run, entries 3–13
lose every obligation and are dropped, entry 2 survives with its own two, and the
review proceeds. Nothing is lost — the eleven were already derived, better, by
the batches that owned them. §12 gives the suggested order.

## What this is NOT
