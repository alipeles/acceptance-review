## What the experiments established

All of it lives in `docs/experiments/317-over-answering/`. Two instruments:
`analyse.py` reads the recorded transcripts and makes no calls; `draw.py` and
`twophase.py` issue live calls against editable copies of the prompt and never
touch the transcript cache.

**The defect is specific to the `# Task` paragraph.** Verified four ways:

- 8 of 35 batches containing a `task-*` requirement over-answer, against 0 of 68
  without one (`findings.md` §2).
- Across 110 single-batch draws, every duplicated disposition and every
  obligation quoting an out-of-batch requirement was filed under `task-01`. The
  two scope exclusions in the same batch produced exactly one obligation each,
  219 times out of 220.
- All 24 obligations ever marked `explicit=false` in the corpus are `task-*`
  (14.4% there, 0 of 558 elsewhere). That is why dropping the `explicit` field
  from decompose is safe; `coverage/open_questions.py:263` still sets it for
  obligations derived from a resolved open question, which is where the
  distinction means something.
- `task-01`'s obligation count is the only quantity any prompt change moved:
  6.58 per draw, then 4.08, 2.80, 3.60. The exclusions sat at 1.00 throughout.

**Splitting the call fixes phase 1 completely.** `twophase.py` batches the 26
bullets with no summary in any batch, then asks one residual question about the
summary. Over 15 trials (60 phase-1 calls): zero obligations quoting an
out-of-batch requirement, zero duplicated dispositions. The single-batch shape
leaked in 14 of 50 draws.

**The residual question works, but only with a neutral prompt.** The first
version said "an EMPTY list is the expected answer" and returned nothing in
10 of 10 trials — silently losing the mandate's central requirement, that
records are made before any test is looked at. No bullet states it, and phase 1
produced it 0 times in 261 obligations. Removing that sentence and one other
recovered it in 5 of 5 trials. Both prompt versions are kept:
`prompts-v2-baseline/phase2-system-v1-discouraging.txt` is the bad one.

With the neutral prompt, all 5 trials returned the same 3 obligations: two
genuine (the ordering requirement, and that later work refers to a record by its
identifier — no bullet states either) and one restatement of `constraint-03`.
Nothing invented, nothing misread.

## Open, and what I would do next

1. **Decide what #317 becomes.** The two-phase split is a much larger change
   than the issue describes, and it is not obviously #317's job. `findings.md`
   §5 proposes a separate, free fix — drop an obligation whose quotation lands
   in a requirement the call was not asked about — which needs no re-record and
   is the before/after instrument for everything else. §12 gives an order.
2. **A hazard for that §5 fix, found on 2026-08-29.** The task file says "later
   work refers **to by** its identifier", which is missing a word. The model
   repairs the grammar to "refers **to it by**", so its quotation no longer
   matches the source and the obligation would be dropped as uncitable — losing
   a real requirement. Fix the wording in `current-task.md`, and record the
   general hazard before the §5 rule is built.
3. **More trials, and a second task file.** Everything rests on one mandate
   whose Task paragraph I wrote. 5 trials returned identical counts, which is
   either stable behaviour or too small a sample to have seen the variation.
4. **`constraint-09` yielded three obligations in one trial** — a compound plus
   its two halves. Different defect, not #317, not measured by anything here.

## The experiment tools, in one line each

- `analyse.py` — mines the transcript cache. No calls. `findings.md` is its
  write-up.
- `schema_cost.py` — prices candidate response schemas. Its table is why the
  one-field-per-requirement shape is rejected: 93 KB of schema at batch size 8
  against today's 11 KB.
- `draw.py` — repeated live draws of one batch against editable prompt files.
  `materialize` / `run` / `summarize` / `show`. `show` prints the request
  exactly as the provider receives it, including the schema.
- `twophase.py` — the split design. `materialize` / `run` / `preview` /
  `summarize`. `preview` renders phase 2's request without calling anything.
- Working directories (`work*/`) are gitignored: they hold live draws, and a
  saved draw keeps the response and the request key but never the request, so
  it is evidence rather than a transcript.
