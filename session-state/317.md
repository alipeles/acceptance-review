## The defect in one paragraph

Decompose batch 1 was asked about three requirements and returned fourteen
dispositions — two clean, then `task-01` twelve times. All twelve say `yielded`
and each carries a **different** obligation: the model split one requirement
across twelve dispositions instead of nesting twelve obligations inside one
disposition's `more_obligations`. `_requirement_map`
(`src/acceptance/requirement/obligations.py:1275`) treats any two non-identical
dispositions for one requirement as a self-contradiction and raises, ending the
run. Three of four batches had answered cleanly and are discarded with the
fourth.

## What this is NOT

**Not #298**, though it is the same crash at the same line. #298's cause is a
verbatim repeat of a disposition with `-dup` appended to every id — a degenerate
generation — and its proposed fix is to compare dispositions for equality while
ignoring ids. That test does not fire here: these copies differ in **every**
field, not only their ids. Not #248 either, which collapses a repeated
obligation *inside* one disposition. What all three share is the abort site.

Do not fix #298 and #317 with one predicate without checking that predicate
against both recorded responses.

## Direction, from the issue

Merge rather than raise when every disposition returned for one requirement
agrees on its `disposition` kind: union their obligations in returned order,
`_unique` the ids as it already does for collisions, record the merge on
`UnusableAnswerLog` so it is visible. Keep the raise for what #217 actually
names — copies whose `disposition` values differ, or which disagree about a
shared obligation.

Deliberately narrower than "drop any duplicate": the twelve obligations here are
real work, and dropping eleven would silently lose most of a requirement's
decomposition, which is worse than the abort.

**Out of scope, and shared with #298 and #275:** whether one batch's

## Transcript cost

Touching `obligations.py`'s **prompt or response schema** orphans the whole
decompose corpus. This fix should need neither — it is post-response
reconciliation — so plan for it to move `DECOMPOSE_STAGE_LOGIC_VERSION` instead,
which invalidates carried *ledger* entries without orphaning transcripts. Check
that assumption before writing code; if the schema has to change, say so at the
gate rather than absorbing it.
