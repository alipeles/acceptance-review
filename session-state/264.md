## Why this is cheap, and what to be careful of

- **It invalidates no transcripts.** `stage=` is deliberately excluded from the
  request hash (`llm.py`, comment at the `_observed_partitions` append), and
  `usage` is a sibling of `request` in the record, not an input to `request_key`.
- **Two quantities, not one.** A replayed run costs nothing while
  `record["usage"]` holds what the call cost when recorded. Label them distinctly.
