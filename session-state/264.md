## Why this one is cheap, and the trap in it

Both are in the issue and both are load-bearing, so they are repeated here only
as a warning against re-deriving them:

- **It invalidates no transcripts.** `stage=` is deliberately excluded from the
  request hash (provenance, not a determinism control), and `usage` is a sibling
  of `request` in the record rather than an input to `request_key`. That is
  unusual here — most prompt-adjacent work forces a re-record — so sequencing
  this against other prompt work costs nothing.
- **It must not enter review state or the rendered report.** Cost varies between
  two recorded runs, so putting it there breaks the byte-identical-rerun
  invariant (M0.5). Sidecar artifact or CLI footer. The same reasoning that keeps
  the run id out of review state in #269 applies verbatim.
- **A replayed run costs nothing while `record["usage"]` holds what the call cost
  when recorded.** Two different quantities, both wanted, and reporting one as
  the other is a false number.

## The thing that will bite you at Gate 2

**A clean Gate 2 is currently unreachable, and it is not your fault.** #251 — the
rating-stability defect — was confirmed at scale during #269: two consecutive
`check` runs where the second added nine tests and changed no source file, and
`strongly supported` fell from **37 to 4**. One obligation cited a strict superset
of the previous run's tests and still dropped a tier. Evidence and the full
argument are in the comment on #251 and in
`dogfood-logs/269-gate2-run4/` and `-run5/`.

Consequences for this task:

- The loop the process assumes — *write the tests the recommendations ask for,
  re-run, converge* — **does not terminate** while a re-review is systematically
  harsher than a first review of the same work.
- Recent practice, set by #271 and followed by #269, is to land on an unclean
  gate **only when every outstanding finding is attributable to a tool defect
  rather than to the delivery**, and to say so explicitly in the PR body. Get the
  human's decision rather than assuming it.
- **Do not chase the rating by writing more tests.** That was tried in #269's
  Gate 2 run 5 and made the report worse.

## Practical friction

- **`gh` fails inside the sandbox** — `failed to read configuration: ... config.yml:
  operation not permitted`. CLAUDE.md says it is listed in
  `sandbox.excludedCommands` and should run outside automatically; that exclusion
  was **not taking effect** as of 2026-08-20. Until it does, `gh` needs the
  sandbox escape. Say so rather than escaping silently on every call.
- `git`, `pytest` and `ruff` are all fine sandboxed.
- **Use a subshell for a different directory** — `(cd <dir> && <cmd>)`. A bare
  `cd &&` leaks into the next call; that happened during #269 and sent three
  commands to the wrong worktree.
