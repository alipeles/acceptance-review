## What #191 established that bears directly on this

Measured on `167-gate2-run4`, three runs differing **only by seed**, over an
unchanged task file and unchanged code. Artifacts in
`docs/experiments/191-discrimination-partition/`.

- **Nothing is lost.** The harness's semantic alignment reports **0 obligation
  content differences** across all four measurement conditions, and 2 shape
  differences. The five failure modes on the issue are real, but on *this* case
  the decomposition is not dropping requirements.
- **Wording and identity churn heavily.** 38 distinct criterion wordings across
  three runs of ~20 criteria each: **8 appear in all three runs, 7 in two, 23 in
  exactly one.** Ids are re-minted with them — `no-speculative-writing` in one
  run is `nothing-written-speculatively` in the next, same description.
- **That churn is a floor under every downstream stability number.** The
  criterion text *is* the prompt for defect enumeration, so different words in
  means different defects out, means the verdict is answering a different
  question. Any measurement of a later stage inherits it.
- **It is not raw model nondeterminism.** An identical prompt issued three times
  at temperature 0 with a fixed seed returned byte-identical output. The inputs
  move, not the model.

So the practical target here may be narrower than "make decompose
deterministic": **stable ids and stable wording for a criterion whose substance
has not changed** would remove the floor without touching what the decomposer
finds. Worth considering before designing a fix — the issue's Acceptance is
written in terms of the same set under semantic alignment, which is compatible.

## The constraint that governs this issue

From the issue, borrowed from DR-180 and unchanged: **do not buy stability by
blunting the decomposer.** #191 is the cautionary tale — its pre-change
discrimination judge scored beautifully on variance precisely because it answered
`caught` to 114 of 114 defects. A decomposer damped into always emitting the same
thing would score the same way and be just as useless. #195's suite is the guard.
