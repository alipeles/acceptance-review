# #335 — split the edit verifier into two questions

## Where this stands

**The split was built, measured, and removed again. #335's bar is not met, and
the issue is still open with nothing in flight.**

- PR #351 added the second question (merged `b348fae`, 2026-09-21).
- The #334 session produced the fresh audit and ran it: audit v10, edits by
  `openai/gpt-5.4`, gives **16 of 21 bad edits refused (76%, bar 80%)** and
  **5 of 31 good edits refused (16%, bar under 10%)**. I re-derived both from
  the raw labels and attempts myself and got the same numbers. Audit v9, edits
  by `openai/gpt-5.4-mini`, clears the bar, but its edits are wrong in more
  obvious ways and its good-edit denominator is 10; v10 is the arm that counts.
- The behaviour-change question refused **none** of v10's 52 edits and missed
  all four that change no behaviour, verifying one of them. PR #367 removed it
  (merged `099dedb`, 2026-09-23, CI green, gates skipped on the human's
  instruction). Verification is one model call again, plus the mechanical
  comparison, which `VerificationStep.BEHAVIOUR_CHANGE` now names.
- `ExecutionSettings.verify_edits` is still **false**, so the execution tier
  still earns no evidence.

## What is left on #335, if anyone picks it up

The Acceptance is unmet and the obvious next attempt is unknown: the split was
the idea, and it did not work. Whoever takes it needs a different one, then a
fresh audit and measurement of their own. The harness for that now exists on
main: `dogfood-logs/45-injection-audits/run_audit.py`, `score_audit.py`,
`judge_audit.md`, `check_audit_scripts.py`, plus `labels/v{9,10}.json`.

Caveat for them: the defect-match prompt changed in #367, so v10's recorded
answers for that stage no longer replay, and re-running it costs live calls
(roughly $1–2 for one arm, estimated, not measured).
