  denominator toward it.
- **It changes no verdict.** The new report section is advisory only. The
  existing mapping/discrimination/strength chain still produces the rating —
  that is what makes the staged migration attributable, and it is why #314 lands
  in shadow rather than cutting over.

  obligation with no plausible static defect. That is #270's shape: an
  obligation true by construction earns a defect that cannot exist.
