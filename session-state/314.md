decides the stage's request shape.

1. **Review-state models** in `review_state.py`: a pair verdict (its way of
   failing, its test, the verdict, a short reason, and its carry fields), a
   prefilter exclusion (both ids and a reason), and a result holding both plus
   unusable answers. New fields on `Review`.
2. **The prefilter**, a new module in `src/acceptance/defects/`. Sound by
   construction, expected to exclude almost nothing, per the queued decision.
   Reuses `evidence/discovery.py`'s name and import helpers, which are private
   today and need promoting.
3. **The two-arm pilot.** Sparse (each test lists the ways of failing it kills;
   absence means survives) against dense (a verdict per offered way of failing).
   Four figures per arm: recall against the current mapping stage's shared
   mappings, mean predicted kills per way of failing as the guard metric,
   whether shedding is visible, and cost per review. Recall below the control is
   a stop, per DR-173.
4. **The pair stage**, `src/acceptance/defects/pair_mapping.py`, in the shape the
   pilot picks. Batched through `partition.py` under a judgement limit.
   Carry through `carry.py::carry_key` on the way of failing plus a per-test
   source digest — per test, never per file, per DR-293. Ledger entry extended
   in `requirement/ledger.py`, which already carries defect sets.

   after `discover_tests` (it needs the tests) and nothing reads its output.
