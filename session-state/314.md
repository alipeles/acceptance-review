## The measurement, in one paragraph

median (0.9688 against 0.9375) and draws below the bar (1 of 9 against 3 of 9),
at 82% of the output — $0.44 of the pair stage's $2.46. **Three draws would have
chosen wrongly:** `kills-only` averaged 0.9688 on seeds 0–2 and 0.9410 on nine,
ending the worst of the four arms drawn that many times. Full figures and the two
rejected arms are in that folder's README.
